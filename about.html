About the Indoor CO₂ Map

We spend most of our lives indoors, yet the air in the shops, cafés, waiting rooms and trains we use every day is almost never measured, and most indoor air pollution can’t be seen or smelt.

IndoorCO2Map is a global citizen science project that maps CO₂ levels in public indoor spaces. Contributors take a portable CO₂ monitor along as they go about their day. The measurements show how well a space is ventilated and how much of the air has already been breathed out by others: air that can carry viruses and other infectious microbes.

All data is open, and the app is free, open source and needs no user account.
